Overview

Asset Portal

For factions that aren't in the list. Upload your own logo, choose your own colours, and generate titles, signatures and headers that match your community instead of ours.

Creators

  • Title Titles and separators in your own colours. In development
  • Signature Signatures with your logo, name, rank and callsign. In development
  • Header Forum headers built from your colour scheme. In development

How it will work

  • Bring a logo Drop in a PNG and it sits where the faction seal sits on the other hubs.
  • Pick your colours A primary colour tints the bar, the buttons and the generated banner.
  • Everything stays in your browser The logo is read locally and drawn onto the canvas. Nothing is uploaded or stored on a server.
